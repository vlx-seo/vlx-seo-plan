### H1
VLX Introduces VLX Home: AI-Native Home Inspection Software for Inspection Businesses

### Standfirst / dek
VLX expands beyond the field with AI-native home inspection software that brings the entire home inspection workflow — booking, scheduling, inspections, and reporting — into one connected platform.

### Intro
Today VLX introduces VLX Home, home inspection software built to take inspectors beyond the field and connect the entire job — from the first booking to the final report — in one AI-native platform.

"Inspectors still spend too much time working through long, repetitive processes using technology built around the old way of doing inspections," said David Woldenberg, CEO of VLX.

## H2 — Expanding the VLX inspection platform
VLX Home is [home inspection software](/digital-inspections-software/home/) built on the VLX Digital Inspection Software and designed around the specific needs of home inspectors and inspection companies.

VLX has already developed technology focused on making field inspections more efficient and proactive, helping inspectors capture information through photos, video, voice, notes, and structured data while reducing the manual work traditionally required to complete an inspection.

VLX Home extends that foundation into the broader workflow surrounding the inspection, including:

- Online booking and scheduling
- Job and workflow management
- Mobile inspections and intelligent field data capture
- AI-assisted inspection and reporting
- Customizable inspection templates and reports

Additional operational, management, and analytics capabilities will continue to be introduced as VLX Home progresses from Early Access toward broader availability.

## H2 — AI built into the home inspection process
VLX is building its inspection technology around the idea that AI should be embedded into the workflow rather than added as another tool inspectors have to manage.

### H3 — AI Walkthrough
AI Walkthrough helps inspectors capture information naturally while moving through a property and transforms that information into structured inspection findings.

### H3 — AI Scribe
AI Scribe helps turn inspection findings into clear report narratives using the terminology, tone, and reporting style defined by the inspector or inspection company.

### H3 — AI Template Builder
AI Template Builder helps inspection businesses create digital inspection templates from their existing forms, checklists, and reports.

VLX is also developing additional AI capabilities designed to further reduce the manual work involved in inspections. These include more advanced real-time image analysis, designed to help identify and understand conditions captured in inspection photos, as well as AI-powered report creation that will further automate how inspection data is organized and transformed into a finished report.

Together, these capabilities are part of VLX's broader vision for an inspection platform where AI works alongside the inspector throughout the process, while the inspector remains in control of the findings and final report.

## H2 — Purpose-built home inspection software for inspection businesses
VLX Home represents VLX's expansion into a complete home inspection software solution for the home inspection industry.

The underlying VLX platform was built to support complex inspections, audits, and verification workflows across industries. VLX Home applies that inspection expertise and technology specifically to the workflows and requirements of [home inspectors](/home-inspectors/).

Rather than simply digitizing traditional inspection processes, VLX is working to rethink how inspection work is performed — using AI, automation, and more intelligent ways of capturing information to create a faster and more productive experience.

VLX Home is being designed to support individual inspectors as well as inspection companies as they grow. Explore the full [VLX Home platform](/digital-inspections-software/home/).

## H2 — Early Access
VLX Home is currently in Early Access, giving selected home inspection businesses access to the platform while VLX continues expanding and refining its capabilities.

The platform will progress through Beta and toward general availability as new functionality is introduced and feedback from participating inspectors is incorporated into the product.

Home inspectors and inspection companies interested in learning more can [request Early Access to VLX Home](/vlx-home/).
