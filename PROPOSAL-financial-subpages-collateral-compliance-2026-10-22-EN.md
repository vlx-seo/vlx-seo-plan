## 1. Sub-page — Collateral Inspection Software

**Primary keyword:** `collateral inspection software`

- **H1:** `Collateral Inspection Software for Lenders and Financial Institutions`
- **Meta description:** `Collateral inspection software that helps lenders verify, document, and monitor loan collateral in the field in real time. Book your free VLX demo today.`

**H2s** (each leads with a subkeyword):

- `Collateral monitoring across every loan, location, and asset class`
- `Collateral management software that links field evidence to your loan book`
- `Asset-based lending software for faster, audit-ready field exams`
- `Loan collateral management software built for mobile field teams`

**Subkeywords mapped** (volume / KD / intent — Ranki US):

- `collateral monitoring` — 20 / KD 0 / informational
- `collateral management software` — 20 / KD 0 / commercial
- `asset based lending software` — 70 (recent peaks 170–210) / KD 0 (core variant 19) / commercial · CPC $15.93
- `loan collateral management software` — 10 / commercial

**Why this page should be added:**

- **Matches how the industry names the job.** Banks and asset-based lenders look for a tool to inspect and verify the **collateral** behind a loan (inventory, equipment, floor plan, construction). `collateral inspection software` is that term, with the right commercial intent.
- **We already show up.** vlx.ai ranks ~5th on the "software" variant, and the SERP is 100% collateral-lending players (First American, Alogent, Truepic…). Low difficulty (KD 0) makes it realistic to climb.
- **Carries the one real growth term in this cluster.** `asset based lending software` is the only keyword here with meaningful and rising demand (70/mo, recent peaks 170–210, commercial intent). Anchoring it as an H2 lets this page capture ABL field-exam demand that no current page targets.
- **No cannibalization.** It does not collide with the hub, asset-verification, inspection-companies, or home-inspectors.

## 2. Sub-page — Compliance Inspection Software

**Primary keyword:** `compliance inspection software`

- **H1:** `Compliance Inspection Software for Banks and Lenders`
- **Meta description:** `Compliance inspection software that standardizes regulatory checks, captures field evidence, and keeps every audit trail ready. Book your free VLX demo today.`

**H2s** (each leads with a subkeyword):

- `Compliance inspection software with a complete, exportable audit trail`
- `Loan covenant monitoring software that flags breaches early`
- `Audit and inspection software for repeatable regulatory reviews`
- `Regulatory inspection checklists your examiners can standardize`

**Subkeywords mapped** (volume / KD / intent — Ranki US):

- `compliance inspection software` — 10 / competition MEDIUM / commercial (primary)
- `loan covenant monitoring software` — 10 / commercial
- `audit and inspection software` — 10 / commercial
- `regulatory inspection` — supporting / informational-commercial (no isolated volume; used for topical coverage)

**Why this page should be added:**

- **Captures a distinct intent that "collateral" does not.** Compliance and regulatory reviews are a separate buyer need — covenant checks, audit trails, examiner-ready documentation — and keeping them on their own page avoids diluting the collateral page's focus.
- **Direct conversion keyword for an underserved niche.** Search volume is modest, but this is a **direct, high-intent conversion keyword** serving a small, underserved niche. Competition is thin and nobody owns it clearly, so we can **rank and stand out easily** — and the few people who do search it are qualified buyers, not browsers. A small, well-matched audience with strong purchase intent is exactly the kind of page worth owning.
- **Supports a topical cluster.** Covenant monitoring and audit/inspection subkeywords let this page own the "stay compliant / stay audit-ready" angle for lenders.
