# GoAudits Alternative for Teams That Outgrow Basic Audit Checklists

*Last verified: November 20, 2026. Prices and features change; links to each vendor's own pages are at the end.*

**GoAudits** is a well-built audit and inspection app. It is easy to start with, priced for small teams, and backed by a support team that will even build your first checklists for free. If you are looking for a **GoAudits alternative**, it is usually not because GoAudits is bad. It is because your inspections have started to carry more weight: auditors you need to trust, clients who want reports in their own format, and data that has to stand up to scrutiny.

This post compares the two honestly, including where GoAudits wins.

## The short answer

- **Stay with GoAudits** if you need straightforward checklists, scoring, corrective actions and PDF reports at a low per-user price.
- **Look at VLX** if you need any of these:
  - **AI** across the inspection workflow (GoAudits has none);
  - **validation that inspection data is genuine**;
  - **custom Word reports** mapped to your fields;
  - barcode scans parsed into fields;
  - inspections without a template.

## Where VLX is different

### 1. AI where GoAudits has none
GoAudits has no AI features on its website, in its help center or in its app listing. VLX includes the **VLX AI Suite** on every plan, starting with Pro: the **AI Template Builder**, **AI Scribe** and **AI Walkthroughs**.

### 2. KYPiT: is this inspection data real?
GoAudits can enforce *where* an audit starts with GPS geofencing. VLX Enterprise's **KYPiT** goes further and scores every inspection across **Image Validation, User Identity Validation, Domain Analytics, Activity Analytics and IP Analytics**. It flags photos uploaded from a camera roll instead of taken on-site, out-of-window captures, and unusual identity or activity patterns, so reviewers know which inspections to check.

### 3. Reports in your client's format
GoAudits exports to Word on Enterprise. VLX (included on Enterprise, on request on Pro+) lets you **build the Word template yourself**, place Field IDs, and generate a branded .docx automatically. Add **report presets per team or client**, plus **Excel exports in multiple views** (Enterprise).

### 4. Richer field capture
VLX captures:
- **video and audio** alongside photos;
- **barcodes parsed into separate fields**;
- **free-form inspections** with no template.

GoAudits is photo-centric and template-first.

### 5. Workflow control
VLX offers **custom statuses**, and **Action Buttons** that lock a page, change status and email people with one tap (Pro+). It also has **third-party sharing with three roles**.

## Where GoAudits is stronger

- **Price for small teams:** Starter at $10/user/mo annually.
- **Done-for-you setup:** "Free checklist setup from our team", even during the trial, plus a dedicated account manager on annual Enterprise plans over 3 users.
- **Corrective action plans:** priorities with due-date SLAs, escalations, approval of closure, and unlimited free external assignees.
- **Geofencing:** blocks audits from being started outside a defined area (Enterprise).
- **60-day money-back guarantee.**

If your audits are standard checklists with corrective actions, GoAudits is a sensible, affordable choice.

## FAQ

**How much does GoAudits cost?**
As of November 2026:
- Starter: $10 per user per month billed annually ($12 monthly).
- Enterprise: $30 per user per month billed annually ($35 monthly).
- SSO is a paid add-on.

There is a 14-day free trial.

**How much does VLX cost?**
- Pro: $29 per user per month billed annually ($35 monthly).
- Pro+: $47 billed annually ($55 monthly).
- Enterprise: custom.

There is a 15-day free trial with no credit card required.

**Does GoAudits have SSO?**
Yes. SAML 2.0 SSO (Azure AD, Okta) is a paid add-on on GoAudits Enterprise. VLX offers SSO (Microsoft Entra ID / SAML) on its Enterprise plan.

**Does GoAudits use AI?**
We found no AI features on GoAudits' website, help center or app listing as of November 2026.

**Can GoAudits verify inspection location?**
Yes. GoAudits Enterprise offers GPS geofencing. VLX adds geolocation tagging on every plan, and KYPiT image, identity, domain, activity and IP validation on Enterprise.
