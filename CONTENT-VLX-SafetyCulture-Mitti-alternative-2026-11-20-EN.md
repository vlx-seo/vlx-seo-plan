# SafetyCulture Alternative (iAuditor, Now Mitti): How VLX Compares in 2026

*Last verified: November 20, 2026. Prices and features change; links to each vendor's own pages are at the end.*

If you have been searching for a **SafetyCulture alternative**, the first thing to know is that the name changed. On **August 11, 2026, SafetyCulture rebranded as Mitti**. The product is now called "Mitti (by SafetyCulture)", and safetyculture.com redirects to mitti.com. It is the same company and platform that many teams still know as **iAuditor**.

Mitti is a strong, mature platform: a huge template library, AI included in every plan, sensors and IoT, training, and asset management. This post is not about whether Mitti is good. It is about **when a different tool is the better fit**: specifically, when your inspections are only as valuable as your confidence that the data is real.

## The short answer

- **Choose Mitti** if you need one broad frontline-operations suite (inspections, training, sensors, assets, contractor management) for a large distributed workforce, and a free plan to start.
- **Choose VLX** if the point of your inspections is **evidence that holds up**: lenders verifying collateral, insurers reviewing claims, or brands auditing vendors and sites. That means validated photos and identities, locked records, and reports in your client's exact format.

## What changed at SafetyCulture (and why older comparisons are wrong)

Many comparison pages, including older versions of ours, still describe "SafetyCulture (iAuditor)" as it was in 2024–2025. In 2026:

- **New name:** Mitti (by SafetyCulture). The help center moved to help.mitti.com.
- **AI in every plan:** Mitti's pricing page says "AI included in every plan", metered in AI credits. Its AI Assistant can **generate a template from a prompt or an uploaded file**, create issues from a photo or voice note, and summarize reports.
- **Word export:** available on the Premium and Enterprise plans.
- **Formulas:** "calculation" questions with functions.

So an honest comparison in 2026 can no longer say "SafetyCulture has no AI templates" or "no Word reports". The real differences are elsewhere.

## Where VLX is different

### 1. KYPiT: validation built into every inspection
On the Enterprise plan, **KYPiT** automatically scores each inspection across **five categories: Image Validation, User Identity Validation, Domain Analytics, Activity Analytics and IP Analytics**. It checks, for example, that a photo was taken with the device camera during the inspection window at the assigned location, rather than uploaded from a camera roll. It also checks whether the inspector's identity signals match their history. Reviewers see which inspections need a second look.

KYPiT doesn't block submissions. It **flags** them so your team can review with evidence. Mitti captures GPS per response, but it does not offer an equivalent validation layer.

### 2. Reports in your client's exact format
Mitti exports to Word using its standard layouts. VLX (included on Enterprise, on request on Pro+) lets you **design the report in Word yourself**, place Field IDs, and auto-generate a branded .docx that matches the format your lender, insurer or client already uses. Report presets let each team or client get its own output.

### 3. Records that stay put
With Action Buttons (Pro+), a field user can **lock a page**, change the status or a custom status, and notify people with one tap. Unlocking is controlled. For audit trails where "who changed what after submission" matters, this is a real difference.

### 4. Evidence without a template
VLX **free-form inspections** let a user walk a site and capture photos, video, audio and notes in a timeline, without building a template first. They are useful for ad-hoc visits and first walkthroughs.

### 5. A trial without a credit card
VLX's 15-day trial on Pro and Pro+ requires no card.

## Where Mitti is stronger

- **Breadth:** inspections plus training (EdApp is now part of Mitti), assets and telematics, sensors and IoT, Heads Up communications, contractor management and insurance.
- **AI depth:** the AI Assistant, scheduled Agents, AI issue creation and AI report summaries, in every plan.
- **Price and free plan:** $24/seat/mo annually, and a free plan for up to 10 users.
- **Corrective-action workflows:** actions with custom statuses and transitions, issues and investigations.

If those are your priorities, Mitti is a reasonable choice. If verified evidence and client-ready reports are, look at VLX.

## FAQ

**Is SafetyCulture the same as Mitti?**
Yes. SafetyCulture rebranded as Mitti on August 11, 2026. The app formerly known as iAuditor is now Mitti (by SafetyCulture).

**How much does SafetyCulture (Mitti) cost?**
As of November 2026, Mitti's pricing page lists:
- Free: $0, up to 10 users.
- Premium: $24 per seat per month billed annually, or $29 monthly.
- Enterprise: custom pricing. Location-based pricing is also available.

**How much does VLX cost?**
- Pro: $29 per user per month billed annually ($35 monthly).
- Pro+: $47 billed annually ($55 monthly).
- Enterprise: custom.

There is a 15-day free trial on Pro and Pro+ with no credit card required.

**Does Mitti have AI?**
Yes. AI is included in every Mitti plan, including AI template creation, an AI Assistant and AI issue reporting. VLX also includes its AI Suite (AI Template Builder, AI Scribe, AI Walkthroughs) on every plan. The main difference is VLX's KYPiT data validation, which Mitti doesn't offer.

**Can I export Word reports in Mitti?**
Yes, on Premium and Enterprise. VLX adds custom Word templates with Field ID mapping (included on Enterprise, on request on Pro+).

**Which plan includes KYPiT?**
VLX Enterprise.
